02 — Capabilities

Why we need smart ambulances.

Highly responsive, tech-enabled ambulances don't just move faster — they close five specific, measurable gaps that cost lives today. Each problem has a solution we can deploy now.

01

Reduce delayed response & increase coverage

Fragmented EMS systems, poor road access and inadequate availability lead to long response times and avoidable mortality. Rural and peri-urban areas are particularly underserved.

What we deploy
  • Tech-enabled dispatch with GPS-based nearest-vehicle allocation and traffic-aware routing.
  • Real-time data to hospitals on patient condition, with live viewing to assist the paramedic.
  • Hub-and-spoke fleet placement covering high-incidence zones and rural clusters.
  • One universal access number and app-based data sharing.
02

Put trained paramedics on every vehicle

Patients are too often managed by bystanders with minimal training, amid a shortage of skilled prehospital professionals — hurting outcomes for trauma, stroke and other time-sensitive conditions.

What we deploy
  • EMT/paramedic-led ambulances with protocolized care for trauma, stroke, STEMI and sepsis.
  • Continuous training for EMTs and drivers, plus telemedicine support from emergency physicians.
  • Community first-responder training — police, taxi drivers, factory staff — linked into dispatch.
03

Fix triage & destination decisions

Teams struggle to identify time-sensitive conditions, causing under-triage, over-triage or the wrong hospital choice — raising mortality, ED crowding and cost for patients and hospitals alike.

What we deploy
  • AI decision-support and standardized triage scales embedded in EMT tablets.
  • Condition-specific pathways: stroke → CT centre, STEMI → PCI hospital, polytrauma → trauma centre.
  • Real-time sharing of vitals and assessment so receiving teams activate early — proactive, not reactive.
04

Integrate, coordinate & make data visible

Prehospital systems are uncoordinated — lacking a unified access number, protocols and data exchange. Incomplete EMS-to-hospital information limits audit, benchmarking and planning.

What we deploy
  • A central digital platform integrating call centre, dispatch, navigation, documentation and hospital comms.
  • Standardized electronic patient care records (ePCR) with structured time stamps, interventions and outcomes.
  • Analytics for governments, payers and hospitals to monitor coverage, response times and clinical indicators.
05

Serve non-emergency & interfacility transport

Systems built around emergencies only leave gaps in scheduled and interfacility transport — logistically complex, critical for continuity of care, and among the most profitable ambulance segments.

What we deploy
  • Dedicated scheduling for dialysis, oncology, rehab visits and chronic-care follow-ups.
  • High-reliability interfacility transfers — ICU, neonatal, cardiac — under hospital contract.
  • On-demand and pre-booked coverage for events, sports and public gatherings.

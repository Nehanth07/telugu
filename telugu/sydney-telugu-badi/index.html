Our Objective

Keeping the Telugu language alive, one happy classroom at a time.

One of the core objectives of the Telugu Association, Sydney is to preserve the Telugu language and to create avenues for children of Telugu origin — and anyone who wishes — to learn it.

We have been running Telugu Badi since 1993. Today it operates across many suburbs of Sydney with a common set of teaching standards and methodology, delivered by a dedicated team of volunteers.

Courses Offered

A path for every young learner.

Level 1

అక్షరాలు

Aksharalu — First Letters

Vowels, consonants and the joy of writing your very first Telugu words.

Ages 5–7
Level 2

పదాలు

Padalu — Words & Reading

Building words, gunintaalu and simple sentences through rhyme and play.

Ages 7–9
Level 3

వాక్యాలు

Vakyalu — Sentences

Reading short stories, conversation and confident everyday writing.

Ages 9–11
Level 4

కథలు

Kathalu — Stories & Culture

Poetry, comprehension, and the culture behind the language.

Ages 11–13

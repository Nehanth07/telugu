    Beginner Batch

    Our youngest learners meet Telugu through play — colours, animals, rhymes and their very first అ ఆ ఇ ఈ — turning every class into a story they can't wait to return to.

    Reading & Writing

    Step-by-step mastery of the Telugu script — vattulu, guninthalu and sentence building — so students can read storybooks and write letters home with confidence.

    Conversation Club

    Weekly speaking circles where learners of every level practise real conversations, build vocabulary and lose the fear of making mistakes.

    Beyond the Classroom

    Cultural Fest

    Annual celebrations of dance, music and drama that bring our whole community together.

    Story Time

    Grandparents and teachers share Telugu folk tales that pass wisdom from one generation to the next.

    Summer Camp

    Immersive holiday programs where children live the language through games, crafts and cooking.

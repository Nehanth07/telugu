Our Story

From a living room to nine campuses.

It began with a handful of parents who refused to let their children lose the language of their grandparents. One weekend class in a living room grew into a movement — volunteer teachers, borrowed halls, hand-made flashcards and endless chai.

Three decades on, TeluguBadi is a joyful institution: structured curriculum, trained educators, cultural festivals and a community that feels like home for every Telugu family in Sydney.

  • First weekend class1992
  • Registered non-profit1998
  • Annual cultural festival2004
  • Adult & heritage program2013
  • Ninth campus opened2024
Our Mission

A language is a way of seeing the world — we make sure ours is never forgotten.

We believe every child deserves to speak with their grandparents, read the poems of their ancestors, and carry their culture forward with pride — wherever in the world they grow up.

How We Teach

Five things that make TeluguBadi different.

01
Joy before grammar
Children learn fastest when they're delighted. We lead with games, rhymes and songs so the language feels like play — the rules follow naturally once the love is there.
02
Levels for every learner
From absolute beginners tracing their first అ ఆ ఇ ఈ to adults returning to their roots, every student is placed in a batch that meets them exactly where they are.
03
Culture woven through
Language lives inside culture. Festivals, folk tales, padyalu and food are part of the curriculum — so students absorb not just words, but the world those words come from.
04
Trained, caring teachers
Our 80+ educators are trained in heritage-language teaching and, above all, are patient. A confident, encouraging classroom is the single biggest predictor of a child who keeps coming back.
05
A community, not a classroom
Parents, grandparents and volunteers are part of the journey. Learning Telugu at TeluguBadi means belonging to a family that celebrates every milestone together.

Our Programs

From first letters to fluent conversation.

Every learner finds their level. Our weekend courses grow with your child — playful for the little ones, structured for readers, and confident-building for adults returning to their roots.

01

Beginner Batch

Our youngest learners meet Telugu through play — colours, animals, rhymes and their very first అ ఆ ఇ ఈ.

02

Reading & Writing

Step-by-step mastery of the script — vattulu, guninthalu and sentence building — so students read storybooks with ease.

03

Conversation Club

Weekly speaking circles where learners practise real conversations and lose the fear of making mistakes.

04

Adult Classes

For parents and grandparents reconnecting with the language — relaxed, practical and full of nostalgia.

05

Culture & Song

Padyalu, folk songs and festival stories that make the language sing and stay in memory.

06

Exam Prep

Structured support for school Telugu assessments and heritage-language certifications.

Good to know

Questions parents ask us most.

01
My child was born here and only speaks English. Is that okay?
Absolutely — most of our beginners start exactly there. Classes are designed for Australian-born children, so everything begins from the very first sound and letter, at a gentle, playful pace.
02
How old should my child be to start?
We welcome children from age 5 into the Beginner Batch, and there's no upper limit — teens and adults have their own dedicated classes too.
03
When and where are classes held?
Weekend mornings across nine Sydney campuses. When you enrol, we place you in the batch closest to home and send you the exact schedule.
04
Do you teach reading and writing, or just speaking?
Both. The Reading & Writing track builds the Telugu script step by step — vattulu, guninthalu and sentences — while Conversation Club keeps speaking confident and natural.
05
Can parents join too?
Yes! Adult Classes are one of our fastest-growing programs — perfect for parents and grandparents reconnecting with the language alongside their kids.
06
What does it cost?
TeluguBadi is a community non-profit, so fees are kept low and go straight back into materials and campuses. Reserve a place and we'll share the current term fee by email — no payment is needed now.
